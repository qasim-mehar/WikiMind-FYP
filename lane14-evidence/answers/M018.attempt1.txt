question: How does RAG-Sequence decoding approximate argmax and why need Thorough or Fast Decoding?
answer:   The RAG-Sequence model approximates the argmax by treating the retrieved document as a single latent variable that is marginalized to calculate the seq2seq probability p(y|x) using a top-K approximation [c-7ad7eab98b64]. Thorough Decoding is a procedure for RAG-Sequence that involves additional forward passes to estimate hypothesis probability [c-c6a43b8b8b2f]. Fast Decoding is a procedure that approximates pθ(y|x,zi) as 0 for hypotheses not generated during beam search [c-ec5d0e88ba65].
