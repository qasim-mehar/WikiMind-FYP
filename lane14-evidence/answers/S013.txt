question: How is training generative adversarial nets formulated as a game?
answer:   Generative adversarial nets are formulated as a minimax two-player game [c-189548848cce]. In this framework, a generative model is pitted against a discriminative model [c-c7bc935b7e9b]. The generative model G maps input noise variables to data space, while the discriminative model D estimates the probability that a sample came from the training data rather than from the generative model [c-23d4ceb2da39, c-4a164079b29a]. The training procedure alternates between optimizing the discriminator and the generator [c-4dac525b5af6].
