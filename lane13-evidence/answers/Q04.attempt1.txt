question: What positional encoding scheme does the Transformer use?
answer:   The Transformer uses positional encodings added to input embeddings to provide information about the relative or absolute position of tokens in a sequence [c-bb24a21a339d]. Additionally, the authors noted that learned positional embeddings and sinusoidal positional encodings produced nearly identical results [c-4eaf30cf91a9].
