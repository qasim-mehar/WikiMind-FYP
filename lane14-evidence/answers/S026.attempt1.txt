question: What are the two RAG formulations and how do they differ?
answer:   The two RAG formulations are RAG-Sequence and RAG-Token [c-9c758c0f3b8b]. They differ in how they handle latent documents during generation: the RAG-Sequence model treats the retrieved document as a single latent variable that is marginalized to calculate the sequence probability, using the same document to predict each target token [c-7ad7eab98b64, c-7f1fd271fc92]. In contrast, the RAG-Token model draws a different latent document for each target token [c-0637c340355b].
