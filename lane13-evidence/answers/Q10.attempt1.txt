question: How is the generative adversarial nets framework formulated as a game between the generator and the discriminator?
answer:   The generative adversarial nets framework is formulated as a minimax two-player game [c-189548848cce]. In this framework, a generative model is pitted against a discriminative model [c-c7bc935b7e9b]. The generator G maps input noise variables to data space, while the discriminator D estimates the probability that a sample came from the training data rather than from the generator [c-2347e3c2ef24, c-4a164079b29a]. The training procedure alternates between optimizing the discriminator and the generator [c-4dac525b5af6].
