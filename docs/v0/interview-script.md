# V0 Interview Script (M7)

Source of truth: V0_PROTOCOL.md § Interviews (questions held verbatim below) and DECISIONS.md D13.
Interviewer: the human. Five interviews, held in parallel with the Week 2 build.

## Recruitment rules (normative)

- Five interviews with evidence-synthesis researchers.
- **At least 3 outside your own network.** Record for each participant: name (or initials), role, affiliation, inside/outside network.

## Ground rules (normative, from the protocol)

- Ask about **past behavior only**. Do not ask for opinions about any product.
- **Do not describe Compendium until the end of the interview.**
- Score a participant as confirming the pain **only if their past behavior shows it cost them time or caused an error**. Politeness, enthusiasm, and hypotheticals ("I would...") do not count.

## Core questions (verbatim, from the protocol)

1. "What did you do the last time a cited paper was retracted, contradicted, or superseded?"
2. "How do you keep a review current?"

## Optional clarifier probes (process aid, not protocol content — behavioral, product-free)

Use to pin down concrete evidence for the M7 scoring rule:

- Roughly when did that happen, and how long did it take you end to end?
- What exactly did you have to change — notes, bibliography, conclusions, or nothing?
- Was anything you had already written wrong until you caught it?
- How did you even find out the paper had retracted / been contradicted / been superseded?
- For "keep a review current": walk me through the last time you revisited a finished review — what did you check, and had anything changed since you wrote it?

## Scoring rubric (M7)

Per participant:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Participant + network | name/initials; inside or outside network |
| Incident evidence | what their past behavior was (quotes) |
| Cost evidence | stated time lost, or a demonstrated error — REQUIRED for PASSED |
| Verdict | PASSED (pain confirmed from past behavior) / NOT-PASSED |

- M7 = count of PASSED participants / 5. **Gate: at least 3 of 5.**
- The network rule and the question rule are audited at H2 against these transcripts.

## End-of-interview disclosure

Only after the core questions are complete may Compendium be described briefly, for goodwill. Anything volunteered afterwards is recorded as off-M7 material. No product-feedback questions.

## Output

- One transcript file per participant under `docs/v0/interviews/`.
- Scores table goes into the H2 gate report (M7) and the final report.
- H2 gate requires: 5 transcripts, M7 verdict recorded, network-rule evidence intact.
